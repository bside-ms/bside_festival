# Helfi signup uses email confirm without per-signup organizer mail

Public Helfi signup collects name, email, phone, and optional free text (no day preferences), then sends a short confirmation mail. After the address is confirmed, a second welcome mail covers onboarding (later Schichtpläne, Treffen, Telegram, festival@). We require email confirmation instead of a captcha, do not email festival@ on every signup (organizers use `/mithelfen/uebersicht` with a confirmed/unconfirmed mark), and do not name Engelsystem publicly—only a later Schichtsystem. Bulk “slots ready” mail and Engelsystem setup stay out of the redesign release.
